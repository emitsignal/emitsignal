---
title: 'subscribe · unsubscribe'
description: 'Manage which topics this account follows. Subscriptions sync across every device.'
---

import { Cmd, Ok, Out, Sp, Term } from '../snippets/terminal.jsx';

```
emitsignal subscribe <topic> [flags]
emitsignal unsubscribe <topic>
```

Aliased to `sub` / `unsub` for convenience.

## Flags

<ParamField body="--min-priority, -p" type="1–5" default="1">
    Only receive events at or above this priority level.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="--mute, -m" type="duration">
    Subscribe but silence delivery for a duration — e.g. `8h`, `1d`. You still receive events; they
    just don't trigger notifications.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="--targets" type="list">
    Override delivery channels: `push`, `email`, `sms`, `slack`. Comma-separated.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="--list, -l" type="flag">
    Print all current subscriptions and exit.
</ParamField>

## Examples

<Term title="emitsignal — zsh">
    <Cmd>emitsignal subscribe alerts/prod -p4</Cmd>
    <Ok>subscribed → alerts/prod · p≥4 · push</Ok>
    <Sp />
    <Cmd>emitsignal sub stripe/payments --mute 8h</Cmd>
    <Ok>subscribed → stripe/payments · muted 8h</Ok>
    <Sp />
    <Cmd>emitsignal subscribe ci/web</Cmd>
    <Ok>subscribed → ci/web · p≥1 · push</Ok>
    <Sp />
    <Cmd>emitsignal sub --list</Cmd>
    <Out color="#f7f7f8">{'alerts/prod    p≥4   push,sms'}</Out>
    <Out color="#f7f7f8">{'deploy/prod    p≥1   push'}</Out>
    <Out color="#a1a1a6">{'stripe/*       p≥1   muted 8h'}</Out>
    <Sp />
    <Cmd>emitsignal unsub stripe/payments</Cmd>
    <Ok>removed → stripe/payments</Ok>
</Term>

<Note>
    Subscriptions are account-wide. When you subscribe on the CLI, the same subscription is active
    in the mobile app and TUI immediately.
</Note>

## Delivery targets

By default, subscriptions route to all registered delivery targets (push, email, etc.)
based on your account settings. Use `--targets` to override per subscription:

<Term title="emitsignal — zsh">
    <Cmd>emitsignal subscribe ci/builds --targets slack</Cmd>
    <Ok>subscribed → ci/builds · p≥1 · slack</Ok>
</Term>
