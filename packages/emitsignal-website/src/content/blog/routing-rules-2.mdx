Routing is the heart of EmitSignal: a topic is just a string, and a rule decides where each signal on it actually goes. The old editor made you assemble that from four dropdowns and a priority slider. It worked, and nobody liked it.

## One box, plain expressions

Rules 2.0 is a single expression field. Write what you mean:

```text
priority >= 4 AND tag:prod        → push, slack #oncall
priority = 5                       → push, sms, slack #oncall
tag:resolved                       → silence
hour between 0 and 7               → digest at 08:00
```

Autocomplete knows your tags, channels, and the fields a signal carries. It will not let you reference a sink you have not connected.

## Live preview against real signals

As you type, the right pane re-runs your expression against the last 500 signals on the topic and shows exactly which would have matched — and where each would have been delivered.

<Callout tone="violet">
    Dry-run is the default. A new rule shows you its matches for as long as you want before you flip
    it live. No more "deploy the rule and watch prod".
</Callout>

## Migrating

Every existing rule was translated to the new syntax automatically. Open any channel and you will see your old dropdowns rendered as an expression — edit it or leave it, both work.

Rules 2.0 is live for all Team and Enterprise workspaces today, and on the free tier next week.
